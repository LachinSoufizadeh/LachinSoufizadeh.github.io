Research

Losing one working copy of the SYNGAP1 gene is a common genetic cause of intellectual disability, and it often comes with autism and epilepsy. In Syngap+/Δ-GAP rats it also shows up as a marked deficit in fear extinction: a learned fear that should fade, persists.

My PhD asks what that looks like in the brain. I work with EEG and local field potential (LFP) recordings from the medial prefrontal cortex, amygdala and olfactory bulb, made while rats go through fear conditioning. I turn the signals into features a model can learn from (spectral power, signal complexity and connectivity between regions), train interpretable classifiers, and use SHAP to see which regions, frequency bands and connections drive each prediction.

The aim is not only a biomarker. I want to understand how the fear circuit is organised, and what changes in it when SYNGAP1 is haploinsufficient.

Findings so far

  • Freezing can be decoded from 5-second windows of brain activity, with 96% accuracy on held-out data in Syngap+/Δ-GAP rats and 94% in wild-type rats.
  • Freezing looks different in the brain of each genotype. In wild-type rats the model draws on both oscillatory power and signal complexity, while in Syngap+/Δ-GAP rats the strongest markers are complexity features, which suggests a weaker version of the usual oscillatory signature of freezing.
  • Genotype can be predicted for rats the model has never seen, with a balanced accuracy of 0.86 during habituation and 0.75 during fear recall.

From my posters at the Machine Intelligence for Health conference and WiML at NeurIPS 2026.

I’m supervised by Peter Kind, Paul Rignanese and Thomas Watson at the Simons Initiative for the Developing Brain, and Arno Onken in the School of Informatics.

Before Edinburgh

For my MSc at the University of Tabriz, supervised by Yashar Sarbaz and Saeed Meshgini, I built an EEG pipeline that tells people with major depressive disorder apart from healthy controls. It cleans the signal with artifact subspace reconstruction and ICA, then uses wavelet sub-band, entropy and fractal-dimension features. It reached up to 96.0% accuracy with eyes open and 94.3% with eyes closed under 10-fold cross-validation, with delta and gamma activity over channels O2 and P3 carrying the most information.
